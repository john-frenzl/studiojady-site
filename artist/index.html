Meet the Artist

John “Jady” Frenzl

John, better known as Jady, spent his career designing things that lived on screens. Studio Jady is what happened when he wanted to design things you can pick up, sit at and set a coffee on.

For nearly a decade he was a designer at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory, and after that a design consultant. The work was careful and useful, but none of it had weight.

So he went looking for some. At TinkerMill, the makerspace in Longmont, Colorado, he learned to MIG and TIG weld, to cast metal, and to cut and print his own parts with lasers and 3D printers. He found that an idea gets honest fast once it has to stand on three legs.

Most Studio Jady pieces start the way the Swirl side table did: a sketch, a 3D model, a printed prototype, then resin, oak and forged steel. Some start as experiments, like aluminum poured into a 3D-printed form that burns away so the metal can take its place.

Today Jady designs and builds one-of-a-kind furniture in the Boulder area, and helps other makers turn their own ideas into wood and metal.
